Kenan Personal privacy policy

Kenan Personal is a private calendar helper used only by its owner, Andrew Bowden.

It accesses Google account identity and calendar data to read calendars and manage events at the owner's direction. Calendar data and Google credentials stay on the owner's own server. They are not shared or sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose models.

The owner can revoke access at Google Account permissions and remove stored data and credentials from the server.
